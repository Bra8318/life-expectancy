Life Expectancy Using Economic Indicators

How to Fill the Form:

  • Country: Enter your country name (e.g., India, USA).
  • Thinness 1–19 years: Percentage of children aged 1–19 who are underweight (if unknown, use estimate like 10–20).
  • Thinness 5–9 years: Percentage of children aged 5–9 who are underweight.
  • BMI: Average Body Mass Index (Normal range: 18.5 – 24.9).
  • Infant Deaths: Number of deaths per 1000 live births (e.g., 30).
  • Under-Five Deaths: Deaths of children under age 5 per 1000 live births.
